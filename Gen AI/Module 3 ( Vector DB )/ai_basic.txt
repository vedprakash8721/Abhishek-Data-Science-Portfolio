Artificial Intelligence (AI) is intelligence demonstrated by machines,
unlike the natural intelligence displayed by humans and animals.
Leading AI textbooks define the field as the study of "intelligent agents": any device that perceives its environment and takes actions that maximize its chance of successfully achieving its goals. Large Language Models (LLMs) are a type of artificial intelligence model designed to understand, generate, and translate human text. They are trained on massive amounts of internet data using deep learning architectures like Transformers.
